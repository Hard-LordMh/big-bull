What you get

Built for research, not for hype

🕐

5-year prediction replay

Expanding walk-forward refits every 21 sessions with a horizon-sized embargo, so no signal date can ever see its own outcome.

📈

Next-week UP / DOWN

One explicit call per session with the probability of that direction — labelled P(UP) or P(DOWN) so the word and the figure always agree.

🌲

Tree consensus

How many of the 100 individual trees voted UP, graded as high, moderate or divided forest.

🧭

Regime detection

Bullish momentum, bearish trend, volatility expansion or range-bound consolidation — with the implication for each.

⚡

Anomaly isolation

An Isolation Forest flags institutional volume surges and volatility breakouts before they blend into the average.

🗓️

Calendar patterns

Weekday, month, day-of-month, intraday window and NSE event cycles — Diwali, Budget, F&O expiry, fiscal year-end.

Pipeline

How a prediction is made

01

Fetch

Real NSE daily OHLCV, up to 7 years, via yfinance. Live quotes from Google Finance.

02

Engineer

22 technical features — momentum, moving averages, volatility, volume, RSI, MACD, ATR.

03

Train

Random Forest, 100 trees, depth 5, balanced classes, out-of-bag scoring on past data only.

04

Validate

Chronological 80/20 split plus 5 walk-forward folds. Every score compared to the majority-class baseline.

05

Decide

Probability of UP or DOWN for each of the next five sessions, with regime and consensus context.
